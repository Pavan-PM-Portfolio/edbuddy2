Before you enrol

Questions we actually get

Do I need any technical background?
None. If you can use email and a spreadsheet, you can do this course. There is no code anywhere in it, and no point where you're asked to install something complicated.
Can I use this on real employee data?
Some of it, carefully — and Module 01 is largely about drawing that line before you cross it. We cover exactly what should never be pasted into a public tool, how to anonymise what can be, and which decisions must stay with a human regardless.
Which AI tool do I need to pay for?
None. Free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini cover every lesson and every project. We teach the approach, not one vendor — so it still works when your company standardises on a different tool.
How long do I have access?
Lifetime, including every future update. When a tool's interface changes we re-shoot the affected lessons, and you get the new version at no extra cost.
Is the certificate recognised?
It's an edBuddy Pro certificate, not a university or professional-body credential — we won't oversell it. What it does carry is a public verification link showing the modules you covered and the projects you completed, which is what most hiring managers actually look at.
What if the course isn't right for me?
Email support within 7 days of purchase for a full refund — no questions asked. You can also preview three lessons free before you pay anything at all.
Can my whole HR team enrol together?
Yes. Team plans include a shared dashboard and progress tracking across everyone enrolled. Get in touch through the For Business page and we'll set it up.
How long will it take me to finish?
Six hours of lessons, but plan for eight to ten with the projects — they use your own live work, which is the point. Most people spread it across two or three weeks. 92% of people who start our courses finish them.
