Why we exist

It started with a question we couldn't answer

We were running an AI session for an HR team. Good slides. Engaged room. Halfway through, someone put their hand up and asked the only question that mattered.

"This is interesting — but can you show me how to do my job with it?" An HR manager, and the reason this company exists

We couldn't. Everything we had was written by engineers, for engineers. She left knowing what a prompt was, and still couldn't rewrite a job description any faster than she had that morning. That gap became the whole company.

01
Generic AI training teaches tools, not jobs
People finished courses able to describe what a large language model was, and unable to use one on the work sitting in their inbox.
02
The missing piece was context, not intelligence
A finance lead doesn't need to learn "AI". They need to learn AI for variance analysis, on their numbers, in their format.
03
Nobody was building the thing we wanted
So we stopped looking for it and started making it — one role at a time, starting with the room we couldn't help.

Our method

How a course gets made

Every track starts the same way — with the people who already do the job.

01
We interview the practitioners
Twenty-plus conversations with people in the role before a single lesson is written. We ask what takes them longest, not what they'd like to learn.
02
We build around real artefacts
Every project uses a deliverable you'd actually produce — a job description, a variance report, a roadmap. You finish the lesson holding the thing.
03
We pilot before we ship
No track goes live until a live cohort finishes it and tells us what broke. If they stall on a lesson, the lesson is wrong — not the learner.
04
We re-shoot as the tools move
Models change monthly. When an interface shifts, we film it again — so you never learn last year's screenshots and find them gone.

Why this matters: it's the reason 92% of people finish. A course built from interviews with your peers doesn't need motivating through — it's already about the work sitting on your desk.

What drives us

The foundation of everything we build

From our vision to our teaching philosophy — here's what we hold to.

Our vision
A world where every professional is fluent in AI — whatever their role or background.
Our mission
Make AI practical and approachable through hands-on, role-based training that delivers real outcomes.
Practical over theory

Every lesson maps to real work — you build, ship and apply, not just watch someone else do it.

Built for your role

No one-size-fits-all syllabus. Tracks are shaped around what your job actually asks of you.

Honest about the limits

We teach where AI fails as carefully as where it wins. Knowing when not to use it is part of the skill.

Current, or not at all

If a lesson teaches a screen that no longer exists, it comes down. Out-of-date training is worse than none.
